## Assets and display safety

The authored catalogue contains 56 independently downloaded club/federation marks plus two national-team flag references. Every downloaded mark has its exact source URL, source page, byte count and SHA-256 in `src/data/team-affiliations.json`. The new Canada flag uses unmodified flag-icons v7.5.0 MIT artwork and the adjacent licence notice. Turkish and Canadian national-team displays deliberately use country flags, not invented federation crests.

The first Miami SVG candidate embedded a base64 raster. The SVG safety test rejected it; the implementation instead uses the pure-vector wordmark linked from the same official profile. No safety assertion was relaxed. Small-viewport visual inspection also found narrow selected-slot text and low-contrast court status notes; the final layout gives metadata the full remaining row and uses light, shorter status labels on the dark court.

Sources establish identity, not trademark permission. No athlete photographs were downloaded into the application. No third-party URLs are requested at runtime. No marks enter PNG exports, and native external image sharing remains disabled.
